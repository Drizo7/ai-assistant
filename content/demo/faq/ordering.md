# Placing an order

Registered customers can order by email, by WhatsApp or at any branch counter. Send the product names, quantities and your customer number.

Sales confirms every order with a pro forma invoice before it is processed. Orders are packed only after the pro forma invoice is accepted.

# Registering as a new customer

New health facilities register once before their first order. Bring a copy of the facility licence and the pharmacist-in-charge registration to any branch, or email them to the sales team.
